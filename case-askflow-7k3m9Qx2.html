Case completo

AskFlow Agents: from personalization to efficiency.

A complete walkthrough of the redesign strategy, discovery process, and business impact behind the Agent Library experience.

Resumo executivo

The core challenge was to move users from a technically intimidating builder to a reassuring and fast activation path.

Problema

Even though the tool was powerful, the drag-and-drop builder created too much friction for non-technical users. Activation dropped and support demand grew.

Estratégia

We identified common baseline patterns across workflows and translated them into a curated library of ready-to-use agents that reduced the need for manual setup.

Impacto

The redesign improved activation speed, reduced support friction, and made the product feel lighter and more approachable.
