Case Study

Retail data triangulation: turning checkout signals into a better CRM strategy.

How designing a clearer analytics surface helped teams identify quality issues and improve decision-making.

1 week

compressed timeline for a high-stakes design sprint.

20 hrs

of focused crunch work to validate and deliver the experience.

100%

of the workflow was restructured around clearer decision signals.

Context

The team needed a faster way to understand retail checkout data quality without getting lost in fragmented dashboards and noisy metrics.

Challenge

Retail data was powerful but hard to interpret. The challenge was to make the experience more actionable for CRM and commercial teams working under time pressure.

Approach

  • Prioritized the most critical quality signals
  • Simplified data distribution across the dashboard
  • Connected data with high-converting decision paths

Solution

The final experience grouped key retail signals into a more coherent flow, helping users quickly judge whether the data was trustworthy and useful.

Unified view

Bringing together checkout quality indicators into one manageable interface.

Faster decisions

Enabling stakeholders to identify data issues and next steps with less friction.
