Case completo

Retail data triangulation: designing for faster decision confidence.

A complete view of how the dashboard experience was restructured to make checkout data quality easier to interpret.

Contexto

The original dashboard overloaded users with fragmented signals and made it difficult to tell whether the data was trustworthy.

Desafio

The team needed a concise way to identify issues affecting checkout quality and to support CRM strategy without extra explanation.

Resultado

The experience was redesigned around a clearer hierarchy of signals, helping teams skip noise and act faster.
