Case completo

Blip usage billing dashboard: turning complexity into trust.

A full version of the design rationale behind reorganizing operational data into a clearer experience.

Visão do projeto

The dashboard needed to communicate billing and usage data in a way that felt calm, structured, and reliable for recurring use.

Decisão

I focused on information hierarchy, scanability, and making the most critical data easy to find on first glance.

Valor

The experience reduced friction for users who needed to understand spend and usage quickly without digging through complex layers.
