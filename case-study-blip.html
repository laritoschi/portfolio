Case Study

WhatsApp usage billing dashboard: simplifying a complex operational experience.

How turning fragmented SaaS usage data into a clearer interface helped teams reduce operational overhead.

Complex

usage data had to be made understandable without losing precision.

Clearer

structure reduced confusion for operational and support teams.

Faster

navigation helped users understand spend and usage faster.

Context

The original experience presented usage and billing information in a fragmented, hard-to-scan way. Teams needed a better operational surface.

Challenge

Users had to piece together information from multiple areas to understand what was happening. The design needed to support clarity and confidence.

Solution

  • Organized key metrics into a clearer hierarchy
  • Reduced visual noise and cognitive load
  • Improved scanability for operational decision-making

Outcome

The dashboard became easier to understand and better aligned with everyday workflows, which improved confidence and reduced support friction.

Operational clarity

Guided the user toward the most relevant billing and usage information first.

Improved confidence

Made the experience more trustworthy for frequent use and fast review.
